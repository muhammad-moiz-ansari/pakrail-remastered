You can book individual shared beds (berths) inside AC Sleeper coaches. You are not forced to book the entire room.
When you book a ticket, you are allocated a single specific berth (bed) within a compartment, meaning you will share the room with other passengers unless you intentionally purchase all the berths in that compartment.
